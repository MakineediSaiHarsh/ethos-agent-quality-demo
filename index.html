Require a payday-aligned billing date and show an automated coverage-to-income affordability warning before a high-ratio application can be submitted.

Credit limits and replacement policies

New agents start with a $5,000 cap on outstanding unearned advances. After 20 policies have baseline 90-day results, they graduate to uncapped nine-month advances. Growing debt or early missed payments can freeze further funding. Sales above the new-agent cap earn commission as premiums arrive.

No fresh advance for the same customer’s replacement within 12 months. Legitimate coverage changes remain available; termination requires human review.

Measurement and pilot assumptions

Use rolling 90-day persistency: the share of policies with premiums paid at day 90. Include early failures, exclude verified Ethos billing failures and reverse associated chargebacks.

Compare against a fixed pilot platform median for the same product type. First-year results confirm the early picture and can block a reward or prompt further restriction. Small books can receive individual review; a few new sales cannot erase prior failures.

The nine-month baseline is assumed. Bands, caps, windows and sample minimums are pilot settings for Finance to validate. Marcus’s $4,200 balance and Dana’s cohort figures are illustrative.
