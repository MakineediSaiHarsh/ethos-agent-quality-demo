# Ethos Agent Quality Program

An interactive prototype of a proposed program for setting commission advances, supporting agents and correcting mistakes.

## Explore the demo

Start with Dana and follow **Situation → Decision → Next steps**, or select any agent.

| Agent | What to explore |
| --- | --- |
| **Dana** | Confirm a billing error to see affected policies excluded, related chargebacks reversed and her normal terms protected. |
| **Priya** | Show her graduation after 20 policies meet baseline 90-day results: the $5,000 new-agent cap is removed and she receives uncapped nine-month advances. |
| **Marcus** | Record repayment to see the debt block cleared while his quality assessment remains separate. Repayment comes from his personal sales commissions and manager team commissions. |
| **Leo** | See why a replacement for the same customer within 12 months earns no fresh advance, while legitimate coverage changes remain possible. |

**Try an appeal** shows how trained service representatives check facts, Quality Operations reviews ambiguous cases and suspected fraud concerns reach Legal. Any termination requires human review.

Open **Program rules** for the advance tiers, quarterly recovery, the ten-month reward and the two checkout safeguards: payday-aligned billing dates and coverage-to-income affordability warnings.

## Illustrative data and assumptions

The agent situations come from the case. The following figures and future outcomes are illustrations:

- **Dana:** 32 of 40 policies are still paying, or 80%. Excluding six verified Ethos billing failures leaves 32 of 34, or 94.1%. The example peer median is 92%; the other two failures still count.
- **Marcus:** the $4,200 unpaid balance is a sample figure.
- **Priya:** graduation is a simulated future outcome, not a claim that she already qualifies.

The nine-month advance baseline is assumed. Tier bands, the $5,000 cap and the 20-policy minimum are pilot defaults for Finance to calibrate. The ten-month reward requires top-quartile 90-day and first-year results across two quarterly reviews, consistent repayments and no malicious conduct.

This is a proposed program, not a description of Ethos’s current operations or internal data.

## Using the interactions

Changes apply only within the demo. **Undo**, **Reset** or a page refresh restores the examples. Appeals are simulated; nothing is submitted or saved.
